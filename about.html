About Us

Creative work, done the Moore way

ExpectMoore Media grew out of a West Michigan real estate family. We learned firsthand how much great media matters, and now we bring it to agents, businesses, and creators.

Our Story

Built from real estate. Made for everyone.

We started by shooting photos, video, and drone footage for our own family's listings, and building the websites and podcast to go with them.

Other agents and business owners noticed and asked for help. ExpectMoore Media is the result: one team handling the photography, video, aerials, audio, and web work that usually takes several vendors to pull off.

We keep things simple. Clear pricing, honest timelines, and work we're proud to put our name on.
